Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 January 2025

1. Who We Are

VirtaViral ("we", "us", "our") is a TikTok content management service. We are responsible for this website (virtaviral.com) and the personal data you provide to us.

Contact: virtaviral@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Business name and contact person's name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (if provided)
  • Website URL (if provided)
  • Messages and enquiry content submitted through our contact form
  • Your plan preferences and content requirements

We also collect limited technical data automatically when you visit our website, including:

  • IP address (anonymised where possible)
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time spent on pages
  • Referring website

3. Why We Collect Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and provide the services you request
  • Send you information relevant to your service enquiry
  • Manage our client relationships and service delivery
  • Improve our website and services based on usage patterns
  • Comply with our legal obligations

The legal basis for processing your personal data is primarily your consent (when submitting our contact form) and our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries.

4. Contact Form Processing

When you submit our contact form, your data is processed by Formspree (formspree.io) before being delivered to us by email. Formspree acts as a data processor on our behalf. By submitting the form, you consent to this processing. Formspree's own Privacy Policy is available at formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy.

5. Website Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), a service of Microsoft Corporation. GitHub may collect limited server log data in connection with hosting. GitHub's Privacy Statement is available at docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies.

6. Cookies

We use the following types of cookies on this website:

  • Functional cookies: These remember your preferences — specifically your chosen language (Finnish or English) and your preferred display theme (light or dark). These are stored in your browser's localStorage and are not transmitted to our servers.
  • Cookie consent cookie: A cookie that records whether you have accepted or declined our cookie notice.

We do not currently use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking. If we add analytics in the future, this policy will be updated accordingly.

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings at any time.

7. Analytics

We do not currently use any third-party analytics services on this website. If analytics are added in the future, this policy will be updated to reflect what data is collected and how it is used.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. We share your data only with:

  • Service providers who help us deliver our services (such as Formspree for form processing and GitHub Pages for hosting), who are required to handle your data securely
  • Authorities if required to do so by law

9. Data Retention

We retain enquiry data for up to 24 months from the date of submission, or for as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected. Client service data is retained for the duration of the client relationship and for up to 5 years afterward for legal and accounting purposes.

10. Your Rights Under GDPR

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access: You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: You can ask us to correct inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure: You can ask us to delete your personal data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to restriction: You can ask us to restrict how we process your data.
  • Right to data portability: You can request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object: You can object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to withdraw consent: Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing prior to withdrawal.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at virtaviral@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. In Finland, this is the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuoja.fi).

11. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of our website after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

13. Contact

For any privacy-related questions or to exercise your rights, please contact us:
